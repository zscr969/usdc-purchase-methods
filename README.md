## What is USDC?

USDC, or USD Coin, is a stablecoin intended to maintain a value close to one U.S. dollar. Unlike Bitcoin or Ether, it is generally used as a dollar-denominated settlement asset rather than as a token whose main purpose is price speculation.

People buy USDC for several practical reasons:

- Holding a digital representation of dollars on a crypto platform
- Trading other digital assets against USDC
- Sending dollar-denominated value to another wallet
- Funding a DeFi application
- Making a crypto payment
- Moving funds between exchanges
- Keeping funds in a crypto account without holding a more volatile asset

USDC is issued across multiple blockchain networks. Circle’s current information lists native USDC support on networks including Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Solana, Polygon, Avalanche, Stellar, Sui, X Layer, and others. The exact networks available for deposit or withdrawal depend on the platform and jurisdiction.

That last point matters more than it sounds. “USDC” is not one single address format shared by every network. USDC on Ethereum, Base, Solana, and other chains can have different deposit addresses and withdrawal requirements. Sending USDC through the wrong network can create a recovery problem, and sometimes the funds cannot be recovered.

| Apple Pay | Convenient if already supported on the account | Availability and fees depend on region and payment provider |

| Crypto conversion | Useful if you already hold another asset | Quote may include a spread or conversion cost |

For a small, urgent purchase, convenience may justify a card-based buy. For recurring purchases or larger balances, compare the final received amount after all fees.
